HIS JOURNEY

A Life Dedicated to India

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and statesman who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. He became widely admired for his contribution to India's scientific and technological development and for his close connection with students and young people.

Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram,Tamil Nadu. Coming from a modest background, he developed a strong interest in science and learning. His early life reflected determination, discipline and a desire to build a better future through education.

He played an important role in India's space and defence programmes. His work with organizations such as the Indian Space Research Organisation and the Defence Research and Development Organisation contributed to major national projects in space technology and missile development.

Beyond his scientific career and presidency, Kalam became particularly known for inspiring students. Through his books, speeches and interactions with young people, he encouraged them to dream boldly, work with dedication and contribute positively to society.

HIS LEGACY

A Vision That Continues to Inspire

Dr. Kalam's legacy extends beyond his scientific achievements and public service. His life demonstrated how education, perseverance and a sense of purpose can help an individual make a meaningful contribution to society.

His ability to communicate with young people made him an especially memorable public figure. His message continues to encourage students to develop knowledge, confidence and a willingness to work for the progress of the nation.
